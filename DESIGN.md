# Hevium design system

The visual system for the Hevium landing page. Values live in `design-system/tokens.css`. Open `design-system/preview.html` to see them rendered.

Sources: `hevium-brand-kit/BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` for colour, logo and type; the pitch deck for layout habits (mono eyebrows, hairline rules, mint on one or two headline words, large stat numerals); Fobo, Solana, Walrus and Tenbin for structure and motion.

## Principles

1. Obsidian first. The page is dark, and mint is the only colour that carries meaning.
2. One accent, used sparingly. Mint marks the primary action, the current item and the words that matter in a headline.
3. Lines over boxes. Hairline rules and surface steps separate content. Shadows are not used.
4. Numbers are content. Stats, tickers and fees are set large, in tabular figures.
5. Motion explains or confirms. If it does neither, it is removed.

## Tokens

Three layers. Primitives hold raw values. Semantic tokens name a purpose. Component tokens belong to one component. Components read semantic or component tokens, never a primitive or a raw value.

## Colour

Colour follows 60-30-10. 60% is the canvas (`--color-bg`). 30% is surfaces and structure: surface steps, hairlines and secondary text. 10% is the accent, spent on the primary action, one highlighted phrase per headline and the current state. If a screen needs more accent than that, something else should give way.

Obsidian is the scheme, chosen by the user on 2026-11-21. It matches the app, so the canvas does not change when a visitor clicks Launch app.

Rules:
- Text on mint is always Obsidian (16.4).
- Muted text never sits on `--color-surface-hover`.
- The brand gradient (mint to teal at 135 degrees) is for the logo mark, the primary button, the fairness bar and chart fills. It is not used for text or large backgrounds.
- Gains are emerald, not mint, as in the app. Mint stays the accent.
- Glow (`--glow-accent`) is for chart lines only.

## Typography

| Role | Family | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Display | Plus Jakarta Sans | Headlines and numerals. Weights 700 and 800 |
| Text and UI | Plus Jakarta Sans | Body copy, buttons, nav, card titles. Weights 400 to 700 |
| Data and labels | JetBrains Mono | Brand face. Weights 400 and 500 |

Both are free with open licences. On 2026-11-21 the user asked for the fonts to follow the DEX. The app sets everything in Plus Jakarta Sans, and hevium.xyz/brand names it for display, UI titles and prose, with JetBrains Mono for addresses, maths and commands. This replaces Sora with Manrope, which the user had picked earlier the same day. The app itself loads no mono face, so mono labels are the brand page's rule, not the app's.

Set aside: Sora with Manrope, Geist, Tektur with Inter, Space Grotesk with DM Sans, Neue Machina with IBM Plex Sans, and Hevium Display, a custom face built with make-font. Its files stay in `fonts/hevium-display` and are not used.

Rules:
- Headings use `text-wrap: balance`. Paragraphs use `text-wrap: pretty` and cap at 68 characters.
- Numbers that change or line up use `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.
- In a headline, at most one phrase is mint.
- Mono is for labels of 14px or smaller, tickers, addresses and code. It is never used for sentences.
- The "Hevium" wordmark is never re-typed. Use the lockup files.
- Fonts are self-hosted in production with `font-display: swap`, and only the weights listed above are loaded.
- Languages: the page is written in eight (English, Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean). Plus Jakarta Sans and JetBrains Mono cover the first four. Chinese, Japanese and Korean are set in the reader's own system face, which the browser picks from the page's `lang`; for them display and title tracking is 0 (their characters fill the em), the eyebrow's tracking is 0.04em (no capitals to space), lines break by `line-break: strict`, and Korean breaks between words (`word-break: keep-all`). Names, tickers and figures stay in Latin letters in every language.

## Layout

- Container: max width 1600px (`--container-max`), centred, with a gutter of 16 to 64px (`--gutter`).
- Backgrounds and the 3D canvas run full bleed. Text and UI stay inside the container.
- Grid: 12 columns from 1024px, 8 from 768px, 4 below. Gap 16 to 32px.
- Section spacing: 72 to 160px top and bottom (`--section-y`), where a section runs as long as it needs.
- One screen to a section (the user, 2026-11-22: "Each section must be within the full viewport. So any CTAs or diagrams or assets all must be within the viewport itself"). Where the screen is at least 1024px wide and 720px tall, a section is exactly one screen high (`100svh`). Its content starts 88px down, clear of the floating nav bar (`--screen-top`), and ends 24px above the fold (`--screen-bottom`). The head keeps its size and its margin drops to 32px; the section's piece is marked `.section-piece` and takes the height that is left, up to a limit of its own, and what remains is shared above and below. Each piece says what it does with the height: the Problem cards shrink their objects, the Stocks ring sizes itself from its stage's height, and the Launch press takes the height while its three figures move inside their bins. Between 720 and 832px tall the headline goes on one line where it can, which gives the piece about 50px more. Smaller and shorter screens are the exception the user named: there a section runs as long as it needs. A test holds every section to one screen at 1024 by 768, 1280 by 720, 1440 by 900 and 1920 by 1080.
- Breakpoints: 768, 1024, 1280, 1600px. The design floor is 360px and nothing scrolls sideways down to 320px.
- Spacing steps: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160px.

## Shape and depth

- Mock data carries no tag. The share cards, the trade feed, the agent prompt and the graduation receipt show made-up figures as the product at work, with no "Sample" label, at the user's request on 2026-11-23. The figures stay modest (a gain under 100%) and every card is on a pair the page names.
- Tablet (640px to 1023px): the page keeps the phone's one column, so nothing in it may be spread to the two edges or left hugging one side of the wider screen. The three figures under the statement stand side by side, each over its label. The three walls are three across from 768px. A block narrower than the screen stands in the middle: the Agents prompt card (31rem), the Earn perks (32rem), the the chains section rail (34rem). The solution's head stands a section's distance (`--section-y`) below the cards above it. Set by the user on 2026-11-23 ("check on table breakpoint, i saw alot of spacing are broken"); `site/tests/e2e/polish.spec.ts` holds it at 768, 820 and 1000px.
- Radius: 6px (inputs, small chips), 10px (buttons), 16px (cards), 18px (the nav bar and its menu: a button's radius plus the room around it), 24px (large panels), pill (tags).
- Borders are 1px hairlines. An accent rule is 2px mint, on the left of a callout or above the active column.
- No drop shadows. Depth comes from surface steps and the scene behind the page.
- A film grain overlay sits above the scene at 6% opacity. So far it is built over the statement section only.

## Motion

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--motion-press` | 120ms, ease-out | Button and chip press, scale to 0.97 |
| `--motion-hover` | 160ms, ease | Colour changes on hover |
| `--motion-ui` | 220ms, ease-out | Accordions, menus, tooltips |
| `--motion-panel` | 400ms, ease-out | Drawers, the mobile menu |
| `--motion-reveal` | 700ms, ease-out | Section reveals, 60ms stagger, 12px travel |

Easing: `--ease-out` is `cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for anything entering or responding. `--ease-in-out` is `cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` for things moving on screen. Ease-in is not used.

Rules:
- Animate `transform` and `opacity` only.
- UI motion stays under 300ms. Only section reveals and the scene run longer.
- Nothing scales in from zero. Entrances start at 0.95 or higher with opacity.
- Hover effects sit behind `@media (hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- Reduced motion keeps fades and removes travel, scale and scroll-driven movement.
- Scroll-driven motion is CSS scroll-driven animation, with the finished still as the fallback. GSAP and Lenis are not installed.
- The hero scene moves continuously. So does each section's visual, calmly, and only while it is on screen (set by the user's direction of 2026-11-22; before that only the scene moved).
- The statement section is the exception, twice. It is scrubbed by CSS view timelines, with no script, since neither library is installed yet. And its light is a gradient on the text, which repaints as it scrolls: a transform or an opacity cannot light a paragraph letter by letter.

## Loading and reveal

Loading states use `ascii-reveal` (0.3.0). Its CSS properties are mapped in `tokens.css`: glyphs in JetBrains Mono, accent and flash in mint.

| Where | What plays |
|---|---|
| Entrance | The opening (`site/src/components/Entrance.tsx`; design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-23-entrance-design.md`). A cover in Obsidian with the Hevium mark in star pixels, which fills with light from the bottom up as a percent climbs. Then the hand-off: the stars are thrown apart and fade, the mark is left whole and glides to where the hero's mark stands, the headline rises line by line from behind masks 70ms apart, and the buttons and the nav bar follow. About 1.2s from ready. It waits for the 3D scene where there is one (2.5s at most) and for the headline's face; where there is no scene it is a half-second beat. Played on a first visit and on every refresh (the user, 2026-11-23: "if they refresh or hard refresh their browser then re trigger the preloader"); any other return in the session gets the headline's rise alone (0.6s); a page opened at a section gets neither. GSAP's core drives the timeline, loaded only when it plays |
| Live data | `AsciiSkeleton` in the `scan` variant while fetching, then values decode in with the `decode` preset (about 400ms) |
| Sections | Each part of the page comes in once, the first time it is scrolled to (`site/src/app/entrances.css`, marked by `components/Away.tsx`). Every head comes in one way: eyebrow, headline and line rise 12px and fade in, 60ms apart. Each piece comes in a way of its own: the problem's walls rise by column and their objects assemble from scattered stars; the solution's pills arrive left to right with the line reaching each; the Stocks ring closes in from both sides; the Launch press assembles in stars and its three shares come up; the Agents clients pop in turn, the card rises and the chart comes in from its side; the Earn cards are dealt from one stack; the the chains section hub lands, its lanes run out and each chain arrives as its lane does; the Trust cells rise as they are reached; the FAQ's questions come in from the right; the final mark draws itself and the coins drift in from the two sides. Each is over within 0.9s and only moves and fades. Asked for by the user on 2026-11-23: "each section on appear to have their own unique animation" |
| Off screen | Nothing. A section that is more than a quarter of a screen out of view is marked `data-away` (`site/src/components/Away.tsx`) and every CSS animation inside it is paused; a pixel canvas stops painting when it leaves the screen. Measured on 2026-11-23 with the page standing still at its top: before this the main thread was 42% busy at a phone's size and 72% at a desktop's, all of it animations nobody could see, and after it about 3% |
| Pixel canvases | Painted about 30 times a second at rest, and on every frame while the pointer is pulling the stars |
| Body copy, cards, buttons | No decode. Buttons and chips use `data-ascii="fade"` |

The loader is capped at about 3 seconds and shows once per session.

## Sections

Approved by the user on 2026-11-22: every section after the hero is a set piece, not a headline over cards. One canvas with no rule between sections and no boxed panels; one visual per section that makes the section's point; a headline of seven words or fewer and at most 25 words of support. The rules and each section's visual are in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-22-section-set-pieces-design.md`. One exception, set by the user the same day after seeing the first set piece: the Problem section is a card layout, after OriginKit's features-04 (https://www.originkit.dev/sections/features-04), with a moving object on each card drawn in the page's star pixels (the user first saw these as ASCII pictures, found them "flat and dead", and asked for living pixels). Whether other sections follow the cards or the set pieces was still open when this was written.

Rules:
- Mint is the only colour that glows. The film on the logos shows violet at glancing angles; it is the one place a second hue appears, and only as a reflection.
- The scene idles at about 30 frames a second for the ripple, draws nothing once scrolled out of view, and with reduced motion does not move unless touched.
- It draws at 1.5x on ordinary screens to keep the thin rims smooth, never more than about six million pixels, and drops to 1x by itself after a run of slow frames.

How the site mounts it, as the build plan's hero task asks: `mountHeroScene(canvas, { reducedMotion, onProgress })` in `site/src/hero/scene/hero.js` resolves to `{ setView, dispose }`, and `site/src/components/HeroScene.tsx` is where the site calls it. The canvas is sized by CSS. Its parent, the art box, must start at the top of the hero and be taller than it by the amount of floor wanted below the fold, with a mask over that stretch. Headline and buttons sit above the canvas and let the pointer through everywhere but on links, so a logo under the copy can still be grabbed. The handle also has `setSpotlight(on)`, for the pointer on Launch app.

## Accessibility

- Text contrast meets WCAG AA at every pairing in the colour table.
- Every interactive element has a visible focus ring and a 44px minimum target.
- The canvas, grain and ASCII overlays are hidden from assistive tech. Real text stays in the DOM during every reveal.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Logo

From the brand kit: use the supplied SVG files, keep clear space of one crossbar height, minimum 24px for the mark and 96px for the lockup. Do not rotate, stretch, outline or shadow the mark, and do not set mint on white.
